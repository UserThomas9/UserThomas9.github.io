01 / ABOUT

Academic profile.

I am a Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Liwei An.

My research addresses the safety and coordination of autonomous systems under communication constraints, nonlinear dynamics, and cyber attacks. I received my M.Eng. in Control Science and Engineering (2023) and B.Eng. in Automation (2020) from Yanshan University.

Publications
My work has appeared in Automatica, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, and other IEEE Transactions journals.
Recognition
CAST Young Talent Cultivation Program (doctoral track); National Scholarship at both master's and doctoral levels.

02 / RESEARCH

Research interests.

01

Safe multi-agent coordination

Distributed cooperative tracking and output regulation with collision and obstacle avoidance for multi-agent and multi-robot systems.

02

Communication-constrained control

Quantized control, minimum data-rate conditions, and safe coordination over limited-bandwidth and unreliable communication networks.

03

Resilient nonlinear control

Control barrier functions and adaptive backstepping for safety-critical tracking, with resilience to denial-of-service and stealthy attacks.
